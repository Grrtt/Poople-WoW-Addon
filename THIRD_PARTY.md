# Word database attribution

`Words.lua` contains the 3,903 unique four-letter ASCII entries from the
public-domain ENABLE word list, converted to uppercase and sorted alphabetically.
The list was compiled by Alan Beale and Mendel Cooper.

- Download source: [ENABLE dictionary mirror](https://github.com/dolph/dictionary/blob/master/enable1.txt).
- Public-domain declaration: [ENABLE readme](https://github.com/BartMassey/wordlists/blob/main/README-enable2k.txt).
- Downloaded on October 11, 2026.
- SHA-256 of the original `enable1.txt`:
  `3f16130220645692ed49c7134e24a18504c2ca55b3c012f7290e3e77c63b1a89`.

The addon uses no code, images, puzzle schedules, or word lists from poople.io.

[Poople](https://poople.io/). It is not affiliated with or endorsed by that site.
